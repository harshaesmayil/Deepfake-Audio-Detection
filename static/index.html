Technical details

The displayed score may be temperature-adjusted; it is not a calibrated probability or proof. A temperature below 1 sharpens the model's scores but does not improve detection accuracy. Check the raw score in Technical details. A clip can fool the model, especially with languages, microphones or voice generators unlike its training data.

Audio is converted in your browser and analysed in memory. It is not stored.
